About 1f2d.net

    1fighter2defenders (1f2d) is a survival Minecraft server with a world that never resets. The server was started at 26. April 2021 making it over 4 years & 10 months old with the same map. Over 25221 players have visited at least once. The IP to connect is 1f2d.net (last updated 6th March 2026).


News - 26th October 2025

    Server migration is complete and server is online.

    Instead of renting out a VM I decided to rent an entire physical computer in a datacenter. The specs of the new server:

  • Intel i7-6700 (8) @ 4.000GHz
  • 64gb of DDR4 RAM
  • 2x 512gb SATA ssds

    • The monthly fee is 2x, but it's worth it for what you get.

    The temp map will be downloadable as well, I plan on making a website dedicated to downloading 1f2d content such as april fools & temporary maps.
